First release of the fork — forecasts, radar, maps, widgets and the Watch app are all still here,
just simpler and calmer.

- **New fork branding:** the app is now Felix°, with a new icon.
- **Standard iOS look:** native controls, sectioned forecast list, and system light appearance.
- **Simpler navigation:** two tabs (Weather, Maps); places moved to the Now screen.
- **Less clutter:** cat mascot and stats screen removed, shorter onboarding, MapKit location picker.
- **Languages:** added Traditional Chinese and Brazilian Portuguese, dropped Turkish.
- **Readability fixes:** light mode.
